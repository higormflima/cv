## Reckitt — Media Measurement and Insights

I then joined Reckitt as a Senior Business Analyst in Media Measurement and Insights. I worked on two products: MROI and Intelliview.

MROI, which stands for Marketing Return Over Investment, measured the sales effectiveness of media campaigns using A/B tests and machine learning algorithms. We extracted, transformed, and loaded sales and media data from Google, YouTube, Meta, and TikTok into the platform. The platform then ran machine learning algorithms and delivered insights about campaign performance.

My responsibilities focused on connecting with users, stakeholders, and the product team to help ensure the platform worked correctly. I also supported data quality through checks and Power BI reports that I built and maintained to validate the data.

Intelliview was a series of Power BI dashboards containing Reckitt’s digital media campaign metrics. Brand teams could onboard their accounts and review media activity using both live and historical data. I collected and documented requirements for customized dashboards, onboarded new accounts and users, and resolved tickets.

Across both teams, I worked closely with product managers, Scrum Masters, data engineers, data scientists, Power BI developers, and stakeholders at different organizational levels.

I built five Power BI reports from scratch and improved, tested, and maintained dozens of others. I also gathered and documented requirements and processes for those dashboards.

**Tools and methods:** Agile, Scrum, Azure DevOps, SQL, Python, Google Cloud Platform, Databricks, and media campaign platforms including Google, YouTube, Meta, and TikTok.
